How it works

A patient journey, end to end

The order matters. Each step feeds the next, and each is handled by a product the group operates.

01

Discover

Search verified doctors and clinics on DocIndia by specialty, city, fee and reviews.

02

Book

Instant in-clinic or video appointment with a booking summary before payment.

03

Consult

Secure tele-health room or clinic visit. Records stay with the patient digitally.

04

Second opinion

For cancer or cardiac diagnoses, KareOptions coordinates a specialist review in 72 hours.

05

Treat

Continuity-of-care follow-up, and medical tourism to top Indian hospitals when needed.
