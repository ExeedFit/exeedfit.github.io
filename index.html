About us

Good healthcare should be within everyone's reach

We are a technology company focused on healthcare. We believe care decisions should be driven by medical facts, and that well-designed software can make healthcare easier to access and more affordable for everyone.

Trust

Verified information and credentials come first, so people can make care decisions with confidence.

Simplicity

Products that feel effortless for patients and fit naturally into the everyday work of doctors and clinics.

Affordability

Technology should lower the cost of care, not add to it. We design with access and affordability in mind.

What we build

Digital health, end to end

From the first search for a doctor to follow-up care, we build products that make every step clearer and simpler.

Care discovery

Helping patients find the right, verified doctor based on facts, not advertising.

Virtual & in-clinic care

Booking and consultation tools that work whether the visit happens by video or in person.

Apps for patients & doctors

Mobile and web experiences for records, appointments and communication between patients and care teams.
